Handles belong to the service and carry no raw fixture payload/provenance authority from forms. Confirmation consumption, title/origin update and contribution insertion commit together. Expose deterministic fixtures for not-configured/quota/deadline/no-results/missing-runtime plus existing Ready/Slow/Unavailable; bounded named outcomes only, not arbitrary delays.
